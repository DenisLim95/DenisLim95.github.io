Research

Representational Adversarial Steganography in Colluding LLMs

Apart Research Hackathon (co-organized with Redwood Research), 2026

Co-authored paper on collusive representational steganography as a multi-agent AI control threat model, formalized as a three-player adversarial game. Implemented co-evolutionary adversarial training protocol using GPT-2 small with a rank-8 latent MLP channel; achieved near-perfect covert signal transmission while degrading monitor accuracy to ~20% after 15 adversarial rounds.

Chain-of-thought Faithfulness in Small Reasoning Models

2025

Designed and implemented an end-to-end mechanistic interpretability pipeline to evaluate CoT faithfulness in DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B using question-flipping methodology. Cached residual stream activations using TransformerLens hooks; trained linear probes to classify faithfulness from internal activations.

Medical Inhaler Spacer Research

USC Viterbi Senior Design, 2017

Collaborated with Children's Hospital of Los Angeles to design and test pressurized medicinal inhaler spacer. Implemented Digital Image Correlation (DIC) methods with MATLAB to numerically analyze fluid dynamics and optimize device geometry through systematic experimentation.
