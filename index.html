I am a Research Collaborator at the KAIST STAI Lab under Professor Seong Joon Oh, and a Research Fellow at the SPAR Research Fellowship, researching how reinforcement learning affects metacognition in LLMs using mechanistic interpretability methods.

Previously, I was a Research Engineer at Arcadia Impact working on Automated Research Alignment, a Research Fellow at the MARS V Fellowship, an AI Engineer at Scale AI, and a software engineer at Two Sigma and Amazon.

I am interested in AI safety, mechanistic interpretability, and alignment research.
