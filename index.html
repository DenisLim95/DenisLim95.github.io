Denis Federico Manuel Lim

AI Safety Research

I'm interested in misalignment, particularly in failures that cannot be caught by behavioral evaluation alone, and it runs in three lines. The first is representation geometry, meaning how concepts are encoded in a model's internal activations and what that structure reveals about generalization and robustness. The second is introspection, meaning how well a model's reports about itself, such as its stated confidence or its account of its own reasoning, reflect what is actually happening inside it (Kadavath et al., 2022; Lindsey, 2025), and how post-training changes this (Macar et al., 2026). The third is multi-agent systems, meaning the failures that arise when models interact, such as covert coordination, and how to monitor and control them.

I'm a research intern at the KAIST STAI Lab with Seong Joon Oh and a research fellow at SPAR. Before that I was a research engineer at Arcadia Impact, where I worked with UK AISI on automating alignment research, and a fellow in the MARS programme at the Cambridge AI Safety Hub. I worked as an engineer for several years before moving into research, most recently at Scale AI and Two Sigma. I studied mechanical engineering and computer science at USC.
